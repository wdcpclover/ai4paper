## Overview

**AI4Paper** is an all-in-one AI research workbench available both as a **Zotero 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 plugin** and as a **zero-install web workspace** ([app.ai4paper.pro/agent](https://app.ai4paper.pro/agent)), alongside desktop apps (macOS / Windows), Word / WPS add-ins, and browser extensions.

Instead of stitching together five separate tools for search, translation, journal metrics, note-taking, and review drafting, AI4Paper keeps the entire literature workflow connected and grounded in your real papers.

## Key Capabilities

| Workflow Stage | What AI4Paper Does |
|---|---|
| **Literature Search** | Search real academic papers in natural language (English or Chinese), filter by journal quartile/impact factor, and save directly into folders or Zotero collections. |
| **Layout-Preserving PDF Translation** | Translate entire English PDFs while preserving two-column layouts, math equations, tables, and figures in bilingual side-by-side or translated-only views, plus instant selection translation. |
| **Journal Rankings & Badges** | Show JCR quartiles (Q1–Q4), Impact Factor, CAS journal zones (1–4 & Top), SCIE/SSCI/EI, CCF, and Chinese core tags directly inside your Zotero item list—or look up journals for free at [ai4paper.pro/journal/](https://ai4paper.pro/journal/). |
| **Personal Library Q&A** | Ask questions across dozens or hundreds of PDFs in a selected project folder with source citations pointing back to the original papers. |
| **Multi-Paper Matrix & Review Drafting** | Build structured side-by-side comparison matrices (methods, datasets, metrics, limitations), surface research gaps, draft citation-grounded review outlines, and cross-check in-text citation numbering before submission. |
| **Chinese & English Metadata Completion** | Automatically recognize and complete metadata fields for imported academic PDFs and organize notes cleanly alongside Zotero. |

## Installation

### Option 1: Zotero Plugin (`.xpi`)
1. Download the latest `.xpi` file from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/wdcpclover/ai4paper/releases).
2. Open Zotero and go to **Tools → Plugins** (or **Add-ons**).
3. Click the gear icon in the top-right corner, select **Install Plugin From File…**, and choose the downloaded `.xpi`.
4. Restart Zotero if prompted.

### Option 2: Zero-Install Web Agent Workbench
Open [https://app.ai4paper.pro/agent](https://app.ai4paper.pro/agent) directly in your browser—no plugin installation required.

## Account, Pricing & Privacy

- **Free Journal Lookup**: Online journal quartile and impact factor lookup at [ai4paper.pro/journal/](https://ai4paper.pro/journal/) is open and free without login.
- **Account & Trial**: Online AI features use an AI4Paper account; current trial allowances and plans are displayed on your account page. Standard Zotero library management always continues to work normally.
- **Data & Privacy**: When you invoke online AI reading, translation, search, or sync features, the relevant request content is sent to `ai4paper.pro` solely to fulfill that feature. Your reading annotations and notes remain under your control and are never silently overwritten. Full details: [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) · [Official Documentation](https://ai4paper.pro/docs/).
